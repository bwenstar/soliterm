# Canfield

A one-deck game with a reserve pile to dig through, three cards dealt at a time and foundations that start from whatever rank the deal turns up. It's hard to win, and luck plays a big part.

## The deal

From one 52-card deck:

- 13 cards go to the reserve. Only the top one is face up.
- One card goes face up to each of the four tableau columns.
- One card goes face up onto the first foundation. Its rank is the base rank for this deal, and the status line shows it.
- The other 34 cards go face down into the stock, with an empty waste next to it.

## Goal

Get all 52 cards onto the four foundations. Each one starts with a card of the base rank and builds up in its suit, going from King round to Ace, until it reaches the rank just below the base. With a base of 8, a foundation runs 8, 9, 10, J, Q, K, A, 2 and so on up to 7.

## Moves

- Columns build down in alternating colours: a red 6 on a black 7. Ranks wrap round here too, so a King can go on an Ace of the other colour.
- Any run that follows that rule can be moved as a group, however long it is.
- An empty column is filled at once with the top card of the reserve. Once the reserve is used up, a gap stays open and takes any card or run.
- Only the top card of the reserve can be played, to a foundation or a column. The card under it is turned up when it goes. Nothing can be put on the reserve.
- Dealing turns three cards from the stock onto the waste, or all that's left if there are fewer than three. Only the top card of the waste can be played.
- When the stock is empty, dealing again turns the whole waste back into the stock in the same order. You can go round as many times as you like.
- Foundations build up by suit from the base rank, one card at a time, with the Ace following the King. An empty foundation only takes a card of the base rank. Double-click a column, the waste or the reserve (or press f on it) to send its top card to its foundation.
- Cards on the foundations are still in play. The top card of a foundation can be moved back onto a column where it fits, apart from the base-rank card the foundation started with.

## Scoring

You get 1 point for every card on the foundations. The base card dealt at the start counts, so every game begins on 1. Taking a card back off a foundation loses its point. A won game scores 52. This matches AisleRiot.

Canfield has a casino past: players bought the deck and were paid back for each card they got onto the foundations. Many still play it that way, aiming for the most cards home when a deal can't be won.

## Options

Canfield has no options, so every deal is played by the rules above.

## Tips

- Play from the reserve whenever you have the choice. Reserve cards are stuck until the ones above them have gone, and once the reserve is empty you get to choose what fills a gap.
- Emptying a column is the quickest way into the reserve. The gap is filled with the reserve's top card straight away, which turns up the card under it too.
- The stock comes round in the same groups of three every pass unless you play a card from the waste. Each card you play moves every card after it forward one place on the next pass, so a card that was at the bottom of a three comes up as the top card of the three before it. If a card you want keeps turning up underneath the other two, play one of the waste cards that comes before it.
- Keep the base rank in mind. With a base of 8, the 8s are the cards to free first and the 7s are the last ones home, while Aces and Kings are just middle cards.
