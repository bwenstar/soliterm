# Yukon

Yukon plays a lot like Klondike, but there is no stock. All 52 cards are dealt to the tableau at the start, and you can move any face-up group of cards, even one that isn't in order. It uses one deck and rewards planning more than luck.

## The deal

Four foundations sit above seven columns.

- The first column gets a single card, face up.
- The other six columns get one to six face-down cards (one in the second column, six in the seventh), then five face-up cards on top. That makes them 6, 7, 8, 9, 10 and 11 cards tall.
- That uses the whole deck: 21 cards face down and 31 face up.

The status line shows how many cards are on the foundations.

## Goal

Build all 52 cards onto the four foundations, each one running from Ace to King in a single suit.

## Moves

- **Columns** build down in alternating colours: a red 6 goes on a black 7, and a black 6 on a red 7.
- You can pick up any face-up card in a column along with every card on top of it, whether or not those cards are in sequence. Only the card you lift the group by has to fit where it lands. There is no limit on how many cards move together.
- A face-down card turns face up by itself once it is uncovered.
- An empty column only takes a King, along with anything on top of it.
- **Foundations** start with an Ace and build up in suit to the King. Cards go up one at a time from the top of a column. Double-click a card, or press f, to send it to its foundation.
- Once a card is on a foundation it stays there.
- There is no stock, there are no cells and there is no redeal.

## Scoring

You get one point for each card you put on a foundation, so a won game scores 52. This matches AisleRiot.

## Options

Yukon has no options.

## Tips

- The 21 face-down cards are what stop you, so favour moves that turn one over, especially in the right-hand columns where up to six are hidden.
- A group doesn't have to be in order, so you can reach deep into a column. Lift from a face-up card in the middle to get at the card, or the face-down card, beneath it, as long as the card you lift by has somewhere to go.
- Don't empty a column until a King is ready to go into it. When you can choose, move the King that has the most face-down cards under it.
- Foundations are one way in Yukon, so don't rush a card up while a lower card of the other colour may still need to land on it. Autoplay is careful for the same reason: it holds a card back while either card one rank lower in the other colour is still in the tableau (Aces and twos always go).
